Loyalty Never Dies

The Loyalty Never Dies Allowlist Bot helps keep our FiveM roleplay community safe by managing player verification and allowlist access. Our goal is to provide a secure, enjoyable, and fair environment for every member.

Join Our Discord

What does the bot do?

  • Secure Allowlist Verification
  • Discord Account Verification
  • Protects against unauthorized access
  • Helps reduce cheating and modding
  • Automates community management

Why do we use an Allowlist?

Our allowlist helps ensure that everyone entering our city has completed the required verification process. This significantly reduces malicious activity, improves roleplay quality, and creates a better experience for all players.
